# Three-lens review

**Book lenses:** Deliberate Wisdom, Intrinsic Inspiration, Reasoned Resilience. **Implementation architecture:** selective independent review and synthesis adapted from Compound Writing’s panel pattern. These are model-generated analytic lenses, not three experts or evidence sources.

## Select and scope

Read the case and action, the relevant chapter cards, and the shared contracts. State which lenses matter and why. A full panel uses all three; omit an irrelevant lens with a brief explanation instead of inventing concerns. A simple case usually needs one direct review, not a panel.

An explicit request for a panel authorizes a proportionate review within the available tools. Do not demand a redundant confirmation of the same request. Obtain any genuinely required permission for additional access, paid resources, or actions; do not assume that the word panel authorizes external disclosure.

## Execution honesty

When the host offers appropriate isolated workers and their use is authorized, provide each the same case evidence and the minimum relevant source cards, without other reviewers’ conclusions. Otherwise perform **sequential model-generated lens reviews**, label them that way, and do not claim independence or that agents were spawned. Never fabricate a reviewer output after a tool failure; state the gap and proceed proportionately.

Use the canonical [review lenses](../review-lenses.md). Ask each for: the strongest supported concern; the evidence or assumption behind it; one concrete improvement; the trade-off it introduces; and what could change the recommendation. Keep observations and interpretations distinct.

## Synthesize, do not vote

Present the strongest shared findings, important disagreements, and any unique insight. A concern repeated by several model lenses is not more independently verified. Resolve a disagreement when evidence or a clarified aim resolves it; otherwise name the value trade-off and the responsible human choice. Do not average away a critical safety or evidence gap.

Finish with a short priority order, one bounded next action, one coaching explanation, and the most useful unresolved question. Use review readiness language only within its limits. A panel does not certify scientific validity or authorize an employment decision. Offer debate only for a consequential tension when further deliberation is actually requested; do not run it automatically.


## Shared requirements
Follow the [context](../context-contract.md), [coaching](../coaching-contract.md), and [safety](../safety.md) contracts. Use only the [chapter cards](../routing.md) needed for the requested outcome.
