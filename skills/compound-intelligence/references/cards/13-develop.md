# 13 · How to Develop a Colleague’s Capabilities

**Framework:** GUIDE — Goal, Understanding, Ideas, Deepen, Enable

**Source:** Webb, *Leadership Intelligence*, Chapter 13, printed pp. 194–211. **Pillar:** Intrinsic Inspiration.

The named framework and its central moves are source-derived. The routing boundaries, output format, original coaching question, practice check, and safety constraints are implementation adaptations, not quotations or independently validated interventions.

## Select this when
Help a colleague develop their own understanding, options, and ability to act rather than simply giving them the answer.

## Route elsewhere when
Use direct instruction when knowledge is missing or the user requests it. Do not withhold necessary help behind questions, especially during urgent or safety-critical work.

## Coaching explanation
Webb’s coaching sequence lets the colleague do useful thinking while the leader provides structure, challenge, and support. Development is not the same as making the person guess the leader’s answer.

## Apply the relevant moves

### Goal
Ask what the colleague wants to accomplish or resolve in this conversation. Establish a useful scope rather than assuming the presenting complaint is the whole goal.

### Understanding
Explore their account of the situation and what supports it. Reflect their actual words and distinguish facts from interpretations. The explanation itself can help them clarify their thinking.

### Ideas
Invite the colleague’s options first. Add suggestions transparently when useful or requested, rather than disguising advice as leading questions.

### Deepen
Pressure-test assumptions, ask what would have to be true, explore alternatives, or use a small ACORN comparison. Preserve their ownership of the choice while making weaknesses discussable.

### Enable
Turn the chosen direction into a next step, anticipated obstacle, support request, and check-in. Use a When-Then plan when it would help execution.

## Supporting techniques and spotlights
Developmental Experiences, pp. 209–211: choose a bounded stretch with support and workload adjustment. Show and Explain makes the leader’s approach visible and explains how and why it works. This package adapts that idea into a coaching explanation after a demonstration. The main chapter uses Deepen and Enable; the index also uses the inflected labels Deepening and Enabling.

## Useful output
In live coaching, one useful question at a time, followed by a colleague-owned action. In planning mode, a short GUIDE conversation plan and an example of non-leading support.

## Original coaching question
What ideas have you considered, and where would you most value a challenge or some direct help?

## Small practice
After a demonstration, ask the learner to explain the move in their own words and try it in a slightly different situation.

## Observe and adjust
The colleague can explain their choice and next step without simply repeating the leader’s words.

## Common misuse
Do not turn coaching into endless interrogation or infer stable strengths and weaknesses from one example.
