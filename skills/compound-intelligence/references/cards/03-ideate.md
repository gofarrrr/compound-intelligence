# 03 · How to Come Up with Fresh Ideas and Solutions

**Framework:** FUSE — Focus, Unpack, Shift, Energize

**Source:** Webb, *Leadership Intelligence*, Chapter 3, printed pp. 42–57. **Pillar:** Deliberate Wisdom.

The named framework and its central moves are source-derived. The routing boundaries, output format, original coaching question, practice check, and safety constraints are implementation adaptations, not quotations or independently validated interventions.

## Select this when
Generate fresh options when thinking is stuck, a problem is tangled, or the obvious solution is not enough.

## Route elsewhere when
First use ci-understand when the problem itself is largely unknown. Use ci-decide when credible options already exist and selection is the bottleneck.

## Coaching explanation
Webb combines reduced cognitive load with shifts of perspective. Externalizing the problem and changing the angle can make alternatives easier to see than continued effort along the same track.

## Apply the relevant moves

### Focus
State the problem to solve and remove avoidable distractions. Do not silently substitute a different problem or decide the answer in the framing.

### Unpack
Use Dump and Chunk: externalize the pieces, group related material, and separate questions that need different kinds of thinking. Make the working problem small enough to handle without losing its essential constraints.

### Shift
Consider the Opposite to test a default assumption. Use Rubber Ducking to explain the difficulty plainly. Apply the Two-Bite Rule by leaving and revisiting the problem when a pause is practical. These are options, not a compulsory ritual.

### Energize
Identify a small move that creates traction. Keep option generation distinct from deciding; test or evaluate the most useful candidates with the relevant next tool.

## Supporting techniques and spotlights
Spotlight, pp. 55–57: Planning for Uncertain Futures. Frame the planning question, develop materially different plausible futures, examine base rates and evidence, and consider robust or hedged actions. Any probabilities must have an explicit basis and be labeled estimates; do not invent precision. Route an already-disrupted team needing stability to Know-Be-Do, not only scenario generation.

## Useful output
A clarified problem, a small set of genuinely different options, the assumptions each challenges, and one next experiment or decision step.

## Original coaching question
Which assumption are all your current ideas quietly sharing?

## Small practice
Generate one option that reverses that assumption, then explain the problem aloud without specialist shorthand.

## Observe and adjust
At least one option changes the approach, rather than merely increasing effort on the original plan.

## Common misuse
Do not present a creative idea or imagined future as evidence that it will work.
