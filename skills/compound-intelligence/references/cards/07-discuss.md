# 07 · How to Lead Intelligent Team Discussion

**Framework:** 3 Fs of Team Wisdom — Focused, Frank, Flexible

**Source:** Webb, *Leadership Intelligence*, Chapter 7, printed pp. 99–113. **Pillar:** Deliberate Wisdom.

The named framework and its central moves are source-derived. The routing boundaries, output format, original coaching question, practice check, and safety constraints are implementation adaptations, not quotations or independently validated interventions.

## Select this when
Design or repair a team discussion that lacks focus, candid input, or flexibility of thought.

## Route elsewhere when
Use ci-prepare for one participant’s preparation. Use ci-collaborate for continuing collaboration across the team, rather than one discussion.

## Coaching explanation
Webb’s team-level wisdom requires attention, room for honest contributions, and willingness to change course. Silence or rapid agreement is not evidence that everyone has thought independently.

## Apply the relevant moves

### Focused
Clarify the meeting’s purpose and choose a suitable problem-solving structure. Establish attention and proportionate digital discipline. A brief check-in may make current concerns visible without requiring personal disclosure.

### Frank
Create routes for genuine contributions, such as Turn-Taking or independent written input before discussion. Use What I Like to identify specific merit and build on ideas, while preserving room for direct disagreement. Do not turn it into a compulsory praise sandwich.

### Flexible
Invite missing perspectives, examine an opposite view, or use an appropriate challenge. Revisit what went well, what did not, and what to do more, less, or differently. Judge the thinking process separately from luck in the outcome.

## Useful output
A meeting plan with purpose, input method, discussion structure, dissent opportunity, decision ownership, and a learning check.

## Original coaching question
Whose view is least likely to emerge through the way this discussion is currently run?

## Small practice
Collect one round of independent written views before the leader states a preference.

## Observe and adjust
The process makes at least one relevant difference or uncertainty discussable without lowering standards.

## Common misuse
Do not count simulated viewpoints as real team input, or equate psychological safety with comfort and unanimous agreement.
