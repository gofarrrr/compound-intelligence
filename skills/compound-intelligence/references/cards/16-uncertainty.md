# 16 · How to Lead Through Uncertainty and Upheaval

**Framework:** Know-Be-Do

**Source:** Webb, *Leadership Intelligence*, Chapter 16, printed pp. 249–260. **Pillar:** Reasoned Resilience.

The named framework and its central moves are source-derived. The routing boundaries, output format, original coaching question, practice check, and safety constraints are implementation adaptations, not quotations or independently validated interventions.

## Select this when
Lead a team through change or disruption when people need honest clarity, continuity, and useful action despite unknowns.

## Route elsewhere when
Use ci-ideate for scenario generation before a choice; use ci-recover for an actual failure that needs investigation and repair.

## Coaching explanation
Webb creates stability without false certainty by separating what is known, what remains meaningful and reliable, and what people can actually do.

## Apply the relevant moves

### Know
State verified facts, important unknowns, actual priorities, and what can responsibly be shared. Distinguish confirmed update commitments from proposed ones. Acknowledge the human difficulty without claiming to know everyone’s feelings.

### Be
Identify real enduring strengths, values, relationships, or practices that can provide continuity. Do not invent a reassuring identity or imply that past success guarantees safety.

### Do
Choose useful responses, plausible contingencies, and no-regrets actions. Make the next checkpoint credible and identify how plans will adapt when evidence changes. Be explicit about what is outside the leader’s control.

## Supporting techniques and spotlights
Talking About Crisis and Bad News, pp. 259–260: communicate what can truthfully be said, acknowledge difficulty, connect to real purpose and resources, and identify practical actions. A genuine apology should not evade responsibility with qualifications. Confidential information still requires proper authority before sharing.

## Useful output
A Know-Be-Do brief, a truthful message opening if needed, the next controllable action, and the status of any promised update.

## Original coaching question
What can you say clearly today, what remains unknown, and what useful action does not depend on knowing it yet?

## Small practice
Before the next update, separate facts, unknowns, and proposed actions into three short columns and remove any unsupported reassurance.

## Observe and adjust
People have an honest basis for action without being told that uncertainty has disappeared.

## Common misuse
Do not invent dates, assurances about jobs, or guarantees that the situation will not change again.
