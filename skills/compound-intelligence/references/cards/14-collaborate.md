# 14 · How to Strengthen Team Collaboration

**Framework:** 4 Hs of Team Inspiration — Handshakes, Heroes, Humans, Habits

**Source:** Webb, *Leadership Intelligence*, Chapter 14, printed pp. 212–224. **Pillar:** Intrinsic Inspiration.

The named framework and its central moves are source-derived. The routing boundaries, output format, original coaching question, practice check, and safety constraints are implementation adaptations, not quotations or independently validated interventions.

## Select this when
Build continuing collaboration, mutual help, and useful team habits across people or functions.

## Route elsewhere when
Use ci-discuss for one meeting, ci-align for one goal, and ci-team-resilience for capacity and sustainable working conditions.

## Coaching explanation
Webb combines shared work, recognition of contributions, human connection, and repeated habits. Collaboration becomes concrete through how people ask, offer, learn, and behave under pressure.

## Apply the relevant moves

### Handshakes
Define a Common Goal across boundaries. Use Brag and Borrow to share a real success and a genuine request for help, making interdependence practical rather than merely asserting teamwork.

### Heroes
Recognize evidenced strengths and contributions. Use Mingle and Master to help colleagues learn what others do well, how they learned it, and what they could borrow for their own work.

### Humans
Create appropriate opportunities to know one another. Past-Present-Future sharing and Open Space access are invitations, not requirements to disclose personal history or join inaccessible social activities.

### Habits
Agree a Pressure Pact with a few specific behaviors for difficult moments, rehearse them, and review them after use. Choose a Meeting Rhythm that serves the team rather than adding arbitrary recurring events.

## Useful output
One collaboration bottleneck, the relevant H, a small shared practice, and a way to check whether useful help or coordination improves.

## Original coaching question
Where does this team need a clearer connection between people’s work, not just more friendly contact?

## Small practice
Try one Brag and Borrow exchange tied to a real shared goal, then record whether anyone obtained useful help.

## Observe and adjust
The practice improves actual cooperation and remains accessible and voluntary where personal participation is involved.

## Common misuse
Do not prescribe forced intimacy, compulsory vulnerability, or more meetings as a default cure for structural conflict.
