# 02 · How to Make Time for Your Top Priorities

**Framework:** Yes-Grow-No Rule

**Source:** Webb, *Leadership Intelligence*, Chapter 2, printed pp. 30–41. **Pillar:** Deliberate Wisdom.

The named framework and its central moves are source-derived. The routing boundaries, output format, original coaching question, practice check, and safety constraints are implementation adaptations, not quotations or independently validated interventions.

## Select this when
Protect attention for the user’s most important contribution, reduce commitments, or review a crowded calendar.

## Route elsewhere when
Use ci-delegate for designing a specific handoff; use ci-team-resilience when the underlying issue is a team-wide capacity shortfall.

## Coaching explanation
Webb links better choices to freeing limited attention and protecting the work where the leader adds distinctive value. Saying no becomes clearer when it protects a meaningful yes.

## Apply the relevant moves

### Positive Yes
Identify the contribution that most needs the user’s attention and authority now. Distinguish genuinely important work from demands that are simply salient or urgent. Use a daily cue to recall the priority.

### Let Go and Grow
Identify work that could develop someone else’s capability, with suitable resources and support. Check actual capacity and readiness; delegation is not transferring an impossible workload. Route a selected handoff to Fix-Flex in Chapter 11.

### Positive No
Acknowledge the request, state the real priority being protected, decline clearly, and offer bounded help only when it is genuinely available. Do not manufacture excuses or imply a future commitment that the user cannot keep.

### Yes-Grow-No Calendar
Review actual commitments against the three categories. Protect a Meeting With Myself and suitable high-attention time. Treat calendar changes as proposals until the user authorizes writes.

## Useful output
One protected priority, one candidate for supported delegation, one clear no or renegotiation, and a proposed calendar change.

## Original coaching question
Which part of this work most needs your judgment, rather than just your participation?

## Small practice
Classify the next five commitments as Yes, Grow, or No, then change one with the affected people’s needs in view.

## Observe and adjust
The priority gains usable time without quietly moving unmanageable work to someone else.

## Common misuse
Do not optimize every hour or treat rest, support work, and relationships as inherently low value.
