# 11 · How to Delegate for High Performance

**Framework:** Fix-Flex Approach — What, How, When

**Source:** Webb, *Leadership Intelligence*, Chapter 11, printed pp. 167–179. **Pillar:** Intrinsic Inspiration.

The named framework and its central moves are source-derived. The routing boundaries, output format, original coaching question, practice check, and safety constraints are implementation adaptations, not quotations or independently validated interventions.

## Select this when
Create a clear handoff with appropriate autonomy, resources, and check-ins for a specific piece of work.

## Route elsewhere when
Use ci-prioritize to decide what to stop or hand off. Use ci-develop when the central goal is helping a colleague build their own thinking, not assigning an output.

## Coaching explanation
Webb balances clarity and autonomy: the colleague needs to know what is fixed, where their judgment is welcome, and how support will work. Ambiguity and micromanagement can both undermine performance.

## Apply the relevant moves

### What
Define the outcome, quality bar, resources, constraints, and deadline. Check the colleague’s capacity and relevant experience, using actual information rather than assuming they can absorb the work.

### How
Ask for the colleague’s proposed approach. Separate true nonnegotiables from areas they can choose. Make decision rights and escalation boundaries explicit. Do not offer freedom and then quietly require the leader’s preferred method.

### When
Agree proportionate check-ins, milestones, and conditions for raising risks. Provide support without hovering. Adjust the arrangement when evidence shows more or less support is needed.

### Learn from the handoff
Assess what the agreement and support made possible. Separate an avoidable process problem from an unlucky result before attributing failure to the colleague.

## Supporting techniques and spotlights
One-on-one meetings, pp. 178–179: use them for progress, specific appreciation, feedback, and coaching or support. The check-in should serve the work and relationship, not merely create another reporting ritual.

## Useful output
A handoff brief with fixed outcome and constraints, flexible choices, available support, check-ins, and a clear escalation trigger.

## Original coaching question
What must be fixed for success, and what are you genuinely willing to let them decide?

## Small practice
Ask the colleague to explain the fixed and flexible parts back in their own words and correct any mismatch together.

## Observe and adjust
Both people can describe the same outcome and decision boundaries without needing continuous supervision.

## Common misuse
Do not frame dumping work, unsupported stretch, or false autonomy as development.
