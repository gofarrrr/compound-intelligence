Your direction

Your next move

Open with the reported event, then invite their perspective: "Two agreed handoffs were missed. What happened from your perspective, and what would make the next handoff workable?" Listen before choosing a cause. Then discuss a practical next agreement with the people involved.

One thing to carry forward

An observation describes the event; an interpretation explains it. Keep the two separate, and ask a genuine question when the explanation is still unknown.

Why this fits

Webb's Factual Feedback, Chapter 12, fits a conversation about a specific reported event. It separates observation from character judgment and invites the colleague's perspective before deciding why it happened.
